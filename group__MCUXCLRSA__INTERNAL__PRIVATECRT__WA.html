#define MCUXCLRSA_INTERNAL_PRIVCRT_UPTRT_SIZE
UPTR table size of function mcuxClRsa_privateCRT.
Definition: mcuxClRsa_Internal_PkcDefs.h:100

Definition of CPU workarea size for the mcuxClRsa_privateCRT function depending on the byte-length of p (equal to the byte-length of q). Internally, it depends on the byte-lengths of the exponents dp and dq, and it is rounded up here, based on the fact that dp and dq are smaller than p and q.
