◆ WMM_QUEUED_PACKET_LOWER_LIMIT

#define WMM_QUEUED_PACKET_LOWER_LIMIT   180

Lower threshold for packet queuing in the driver. When the number of packets drops beneath the lower limit after having reached the upper limit, the driver will restart the net queue.

◆ WMM_QUEUED_PACKET_UPPER_LIMIT

#define WMM_QUEUED_PACKET_UPPER_LIMIT   200

Upper threshold for packet queuing in the driver. When the number of packets queued reaches the upper limit, the driver will stop the net queue in the app/kernel space.
