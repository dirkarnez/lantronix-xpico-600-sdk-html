uint16_t mcuxClOsccaSm2_Encrypt_Param_t::options

Two bytes to pass further options to this function:

  • bits 0-3: rfu
  • bit 4:
    • 0 INIT phase will not be performed
    • 1 INIT phase will be performed (C1 is returned via pC1)
  • bit 5:
    • 0 UPDATE phase will not be performed
    • 1 UPDATE phase will be performed (The ciphertext part corresponding to the passed plaintext is returned via pOutput)
  • bit 6:
    • 0 FINALIZE phase will not be performed
    • 1 FINALIZE phase will be performed (C3 is returned via pC3)
  • other bits: rfu

◆ options [3/3]

uint16_t mcuxClOsccaSm2_KeyExchange_Param_t::options

Two bytes to pass further options for this function:

  • bits 0-3: rfu
  • bit 4-5:
    • 01: Role of local party is initiator, i.e., Alice
    • 10: Role of local party is responder, i.e., Bob
  • bit 6-7:
    • 01: Key confirmation shall be computed
    • 10: Key confirmation is skipped
  • other bits: rfu
