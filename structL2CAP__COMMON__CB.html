UCHAR L2CAP_COMMON_CB::dummy

If both L2CAP_HAVE_PING_INFO_SUPPORT and L2CAP_TX_COMPLETE_CALLBACK are not defined compiler will generate warnings due to the empty structure. To avoid this a dummy variable is declared, which serves no purpose.
